# Troubleshooting Guide

Common issues and solutions when working with VeilSplit:

## 1. Freighter Wallet Connection Fails
- Ensure the Freighter extension is active and unlocked in your browser.
- Verify that your wallet is configured to **Testnet** (not Mainnet or Custom).
- If your balance shows 0 XLM, fund your address using the Friendbot faucet.

## 2. Claiming Split Fails
- Confirm that you are signing the transaction with the correct stealth private key generated for that split.
- Verify that your wallet has enough native XLM to cover network fees.
- Check if the bill status is marked as settled.
